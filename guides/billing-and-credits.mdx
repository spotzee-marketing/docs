This guide covers billing for existing Spotzee accounts. Check **Billing** for your subscription, user limit and monthly cost. Platform credits cover usage-based work.

<Info>
The **Billing** page is gated to organisation `admin` and `owner` roles. Members can't see it.
</Info>

## How billing works

Two things to know up front:

- **Billing is at the organisation level**, not per project. Your user count totals across every project in your organisation.
- **Users** are the people you message: customers, subscribers, app users.

For existing accounts on user-based plans, your monthly subscription scales with your user tier. Platform credits are a separate top-up balance you draw on for usage-based features.

## Subscription tiers

The **Billing** page shows your account's plan, user limit and cost. For current pricing, [contact us](https://spotzee.com/contact/).

For existing accounts on user-based plans, the slider shows the matching tier and monthly cost for a selected user count. Self-service checkout runs up to **25,000 users**. Above that, the slider switches to **Let's talk**.

## Manage your subscription

The **Billing** page in the organisation menu is the single place to view, change, or cancel your plan.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="View your plan">

The status card at the top shows:

- Current tier.
- Current user count vs limit (for example, `150 / 250 users`).
- Monthly cost on paid tiers.
- A progress bar of usage against the limit.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Upgrade">

When you're approaching your limit or want headroom:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Billing">
From the organisation menu, open **Billing**.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a new user limit">
Use the slider to choose your new limit. The page shows the matching tier and monthly cost.
</Step>

<Step title="Check out">
Choose **Upgrade now** or **Change plan**, depending on your current plan. Complete payment and confirm the new limit on the Billing page.
</Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Update payment or invoices">

Choose **Manage billing** on the Billing page. You'll be taken to the billing portal where you can:

- Update your card.
- Download past invoices and receipts.
- Adjust subscription details.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Downgrade">

The plan selector on the Billing page supports increases to your user limit. For other subscription changes, choose **Manage billing** to review the available options.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Cancel">

Choose **Manage billing** to open the billing portal and review its available cancellation options. The portal shows when a cancellation takes effect. Contact support if you need to confirm access to your data after cancellation.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## User limit alerts

The Billing page surfaces three alert states based on your usage progress bar.

| Usage | What you see |
|---|---|
| Below 90% | Normal. No alert. |
| 90–99% | Amber warning: approaching your user limit. |
| 100% and above | Red warning: user limit reached. |

When capacity enforcement is enabled for your organisation, new users cannot be added at the limit. The Billing page shows a warning and an upgrade path.

### Payment failures and the grace period

If a card payment fails:

- A 7-day grace period begins.
- A red banner appears on the Billing page.
- You'll receive an email about the failed payment.
- Your account functions normally during the grace window.

If payment isn't resolved within 7 days, your user limit drops to 0 and feature access is restricted until you fix the payment in the billing portal.

## Platform credits

Credits are a pay-as-you-go monetary balance that supplements your subscription. They cover:

- Additional messaging capacity beyond your subscription.
- Per-use operations on certain Extended API utilities (for example, blacklist-monitoring checks, deliverability lookups).
- Usage-based work billed per call.

The **Credit Balance** card on the Billing page shows:

- Your current balance (for example, `$250.00`).
- Total **Purchased**.
- Total **Consumed**.

When the balance drops below **\$10**, the card turns amber as a low-balance warning.

## Top up credits manually

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Credit Balance card">
On the Billing page, scroll to the **Credit Balance** section.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a top-up amount">
Choose from the dropdown: **\$20**, **\$50**, **\$100**, **\$500**, **\$1,000**, or **\$5,000**.
</Step>

<Step title="Top up">
Choose **Top up now**, complete payment, and your balance updates immediately. The transaction lands in your credit history.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Top-ups use the same payment method as your subscription. To change cards, update them in the billing portal first, then return to top up.
</Note>

## Configure auto top-up

Auto top-up keeps your balance topped up automatically when it drops below a threshold you set. Your monthly limit caps total auto spending so the system can never exceed what you've authorised.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open auto top-up configuration">
On the Billing page, choose **Configure** in the Credit Balance section.
</Step>

<Step title="Enable and set values">
Toggle **Enable auto top-up** on, then set:

- **Threshold**: trigger when balance drops below this. Options: \$50, \$100, \$200, \$500.
- **Amount**: how much to add each time. Options: \$100, \$200, \$500, \$1,000, \$2,000.
- **Monthly limit**: the cap on total auto top-ups in a calendar month. Options: \$500, \$1,000, \$2,000, \$5,000, \$10,000.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Save. The Billing page now shows auto top-up as **Active**, with the threshold, monthly limit, and amount used this month against the limit.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Example configuration

| Setting | Value | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | \$100 | Auto top-up triggers when balance falls below \$100 |
| Amount | \$500 | Each top-up adds \$500 |
| Monthly limit | \$2,000 | Auto top-ups stop once \$2,000 has been added in a month |

With these settings, a balance of \$95 triggers a \$500 top-up that lifts it to \$595.

### Credit history

The credit history view on the Billing page lists every transaction with timestamps, amounts, and the balance before and after. Auto top-ups appear with an **Auto top-up** label so you can tell them apart from manual purchases and usage deductions.
