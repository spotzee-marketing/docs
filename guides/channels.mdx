Spotzee sends across five channels. Each channel uses a configured **provider** to do the actual delivery.

## Email

Transactional and marketing email through a configured provider. Your organisation may also have managed delivery configured.

## SMS

Text messaging via your configured SMS provider. Per-message pricing applies on top of platform pricing.

## Push

Mobile push notifications via the standard mobile platforms.

## In-app

Messages rendered inside your own product, served via the API.

## Webhooks

Outbound HTTP callbacks. Use webhooks to forward events to your own systems, trigger third-party integrations, or update CRMs.

## Provider configuration

Providers are configured per project. A project can hold multiple providers per channel. Choose the provider when configuring a campaign. Journey sends use the provider selected for that campaign.
