Three different actions, three different outcomes. Pick the right one for what you're trying to do.

| Action | What happens to data | Status after | Reversible |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Disconnect** | Imported data preserved. Sync paused. | Disconnected (grey) | Yes, by reconnecting |
| **Delete** | Integration and Shopify-specific records removed; Spotzee contacts remain. | Removed | No |
| **Reconnect** | Same data preserved. OAuth refreshed. | Connected (green) | n/a |

## Disconnect

**What it does.** Pauses the integration. Webhooks stop firing into Spotzee, scheduled syncs stop running. Your already-imported customer records, orders, and product data stay in Spotzee.

**Use it when:**

- You're temporarily pausing syncs (during a store migration, a Shopify plan change, or a long debug session).
- You're troubleshooting and want to stop new data flowing while you investigate.
- You're not sure whether you want to fully remove the integration but want to stop new activity.

**How:**

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the store card menu">
Inside the project, open **Integrations** and find the store card.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose Disconnect">
Use the card menu and pick **Disconnect**. Confirm at the prompt: "Are you sure you want to disconnect? You can reconnect later."
</Step>
</Steps>

After disconnect, the status badge flips to **Disconnected** (grey). The store remains in your integration list. Reconnect later from the same menu when you're ready.

## Delete

**What it does.** Removes the integration and its Shopify-specific orders, checkouts, products, collections, sync settings and logs. Shopify fields are cleared from Spotzee contacts, but the contacts themselves remain. The integration card disappears from your **Integrations** page.

**Use it when:**

- You're permanently moving away from a store (closing the store, switching platforms).
- You connected the store to the wrong project and want to remove its integration data.
- The store has been replaced by a different Shopify store at the same domain (rare, but happens with rebrands).

**Be careful.** Delete is destructive and irreversible. If you might want this data back, disconnect instead and decide later.

**How:**

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the store card menu">
Use the card menu on the store you want to remove.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose Delete">
Pick **Delete**. Confirm the destructive action prompt explicitly. Spotzee describes what gets removed before you confirm.
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for cleanup">
Large stores can take time to clear Shopify-specific records. The card disappears once cleanup completes.
</Step>
</Steps>

To restore Shopify-specific records after deletion, connect the store again and run an initial sync. Existing Spotzee contacts remain separate from that new integration.

## Reconnect

**What it does.** Refreshes the OAuth grant. Use it when status shows **Reconnect required** (amber badge) or the integration reports missing scopes.

**Sync settings, customer list assignment, history, and existing data are all preserved.** Only the OAuth credentials change.

**Use it when:**

- Status is **Reconnect required**.
- You uninstalled and reinstalled the Spotzee app in Shopify.
- The integration reports missing scopes.
- The connecting Shopify user's permissions changed.

**How:**

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the store card menu">
Use the card menu and pick **Reconnect**.
</Step>

<Step title="Re-approve on Shopify">
You're sent through the same OAuth flow as the initial connect. Re-approve permissions on Shopify.
</Step>

<Step title="Land back in Spotzee">
On approval, the store flips back to **Connected** (green) and syncs resume.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Picking the right action

A simple decision tree:

- **Want to stop syncing temporarily?** Disconnect. Reversible.
- **Want to fix an OAuth or scope issue?** Reconnect. No data loss.
- **Want to permanently remove the integration and its Shopify-specific data?** Delete. Irreversible.

When in doubt, disconnect. You can always come back.
