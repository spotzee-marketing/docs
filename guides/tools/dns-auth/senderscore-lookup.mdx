Use the sender score lookup to inspect reputation data for a sending IPv4 address. The response includes a score from 0 to 100, a rating and a volume tier. A historical trend and certification flag may also be present. A score is one signal to investigate alongside your bounce rate, complaint rate, authentication and blacklist results; it does not guarantee inbox placement.

<Info>
Run a one-off lookup at [spotzee.com/tools/senderscore-lookup](https://spotzee.com/tools/senderscore-lookup/). Extended API calls use your Spotzee credit balance; see [pricing](https://spotzee.com/pricing/) for current rates.
</Info>

## Check an address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the sending IP">
    Enter an IPv4 address used to send your mail. The lookup does not accept IPv6 addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Read the score, rating and volume tier. Check the historical trend and certification flag when present. A low score or falling trend is a prompt to investigate the sending activity behind that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare other signals">
    Check recent bounce and complaint rates, authentication and [IP blacklist status](/guides/tools/blacklist-security/ip-blacklist-check) before changing your sending plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If `volume_tier` is `INSUFFICIENT`, treat the score as limited evidence and compare it with your own delivery metrics.
