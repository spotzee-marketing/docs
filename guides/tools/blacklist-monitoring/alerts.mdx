title: "Understand blacklist alerts"
description: "When blacklist checks produce email digests and where notifications go."
keywords: ["blacklist monitoring", "alerts", "notifications", "digest"]
---

Blacklist Monitoring checks resources on their group's daily or weekly schedule. You can also choose **Check now** on one resource or **Check all** from the **Actions** menu. A check that finds a listing updates the resource to **Blacklisted**. An hourly notification run groups pending blacklisted check results into digest emails. A resource that remains listed may appear in another digest after a later check.

## Choose recipients

Set **Notification emails** on a group to send its digest to those addresses. When a group has no recipients, Spotzee uses the default notification emails under **Settings**. Ungrouped resources also use the default list. If neither list has recipients, the check still runs and appears in the Tool, but no email is sent.

## Respond to a listing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the affected resource">
    Open **IPs** or **Domains**. Hover over the blacklist count to see which lists reported the resource.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate the cause">
    Check recent sending activity, bounces, complaints and authentication. A blacklist listing alone does not identify its cause.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the list's removal process">
    After addressing the cause, use the reporting blacklist's own instructions to request removal if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check again">
    Choose **Check now** on the resource after removal. A clean result changes its status to **Clean**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To pause checks, use **Disable all** in the **IPs** or **Domains** tab. There is no global pause switch in **Settings**.
