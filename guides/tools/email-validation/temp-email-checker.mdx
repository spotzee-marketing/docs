Use the disposable email checker to flag addresses or domains that fail its known-domain and address checks. It does not contact a mailbox or send a test message. A negative result is not proof that an address is permanent or deliverable.

Open the [disposable email checker](https://spotzee.com/tools/temp-email-checker/). Extended API calls use your Spotzee credit balance; see [pricing](https://spotzee.com/pricing/) for current rates.

## Check an address or domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the input">
    Provide a complete address such as `person@example.com` or a bare domain such as `example.com`. The API accepts either form in `email_domain`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the flag">
    Read `data.isTemporaryEmail`. A `true` result flags the input; it can reflect a known disposable domain or a failed address-format check. Validate the input before treating the flag as a domain reputation verdict.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow a correction">
    If the check blocks a signup or import, give the contact a way to correct the address or request a review. Newly created disposable domains may not yet be recognised.
  </Step>
</Steps>
