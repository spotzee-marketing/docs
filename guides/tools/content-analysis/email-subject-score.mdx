Use the subject line scorer to review a draft against 14 text signals. It returns a percentage, the points earned, and a breakdown for each signal. Treat the score as editing feedback, not a prediction of inbox placement or open rate.

<Info>
Try a draft at [spotzee.com/tools/email-subject-score](https://spotzee.com/tools/email-subject-score).
</Info>

## What the score includes

The scorer checks Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch-Kincaid grade, character and word counts, sentiment, `RE` and `FWD` occurrences, the word `free`, listed spam words, exclamation marks, all-capital words, punctuation, `[FNAME]`, and emoji. Each signal contributes up to 10 points. The result includes `obtainedScore`, `totalCritereaScore` (140), `finalScorePercentage`, and `finalIndividualScore`.

The preferred ranges come from the scorer's rules. For example, it awards full points for 38–53 characters and six to eight words. A low score identifies a mismatch with those rules. It does not show whether a mailbox provider accepted the message or whether recipients opened it.

## Score a subject line

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your draft">
    Submit the subject line, including any punctuation, emoji, or `[FNAME]` token you plan to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the breakdown">
    Inspect each signal's `actualValue` and `score` in `finalIndividualScore` before revising the text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your revision">
    Score the revised line again and compare the signals. Confirm the final wording and personalisation token match your sending platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The `[FNAME]` signal recognises that exact token. If your sending platform uses another format, test with `[FNAME]` only as a scoring proxy and restore your platform's token before sending.

For alternatives to your draft, see the [subject line suggester](/guides/tools/content-analysis/email-subject-suggest). The scorer is also available through the Spotzee Extended API; see [pricing](https://spotzee.com/pricing/) for the current API rate.
